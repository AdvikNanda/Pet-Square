# Pet-Square
A Connected Digital Ecosystem for Pet Owners

Overview

Pet Square is a web-based platform that brings pet owners into a single, unified digital community. It functions as a digital town square where pet parents can connect, seek support, discover adoption opportunities, and manage their pets in one place.

Problem Statement

Pet owners lack a dedicated platform built specifically for their needs. Existing solutions are fragmented across general social media, making it difficult to access trusted community support, adoption resources, and organized pet information.

Our Solution

Pet Square provides a centralized, pet-first ecosystem that combines:
- A focused pet-owner community  
- Adoption listings and resources  
- Personalized pet management  
- AI-powered assistance  

All delivered through a clean, intuitive, web-based interface.

Key Features:

Community Hub:  
Pet-focused posts, Q&A, events, and community support

My Pets:  
Individual pet profiles for organized and personalized management

Adoption Centers:
Centralized adoption listings to encourage responsible pet ownership

AI Assistance:  
Generative AI used for guidance and recommendations while prioritizing safety and human decision-making

Design and Usability:

The platform features a modern, beginner-friendly interface with simple navigation, designed to encourage organic community growth and regular engagement.

Use of Generative AI:

Generative AI enhances usability by providing contextual assistance and quick guidance for both new and experienced pet owners. AI is used responsibly to support decisions without replacing human judgment.

Tech Stack:
